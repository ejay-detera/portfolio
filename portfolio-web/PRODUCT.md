## Product Principles

1. **Artifact Over Ornament**: Real systems, schemas, and architectural decisions lead; delete decorative filler, spinning badges, and generic SaaS widgets.
2. **Zero-Friction Scanning**: Never hide 4–8 items inside a pagination carousel when a structured grid or index lets a recruiter read them in one scroll.
3. **Typography & Hierarchy as Structure**: Use clear typographic contrast (display, readable prose, and restrained monospace metadata) instead of neon borders and glowing boxes.
